One save shared with the other Celeste site

Celeste, Spring Collab 2020, and your other Celeste browser site use the same save files on this device in this browser. Close the game before switching sites. Saves stay in your browser and are not sent to this website or shared with other players. To continue on another device, you would need to move a backup there.

Separate existing saves are backed up before migration; if both have different copies of the same file, the more recently saved version is selected. Download a backup of your browser saves to this PC.

First launch needs around 2 GB of browser storage and downloads the game plus required Spring Collab files. The first Spring Collab startup can take several minutes; the counter may stay at 0/1 while mods initialize, so keep the tab open and do not refresh. If Everest asks whether to play mods, click the game and press C. At the Celeste intro, press Enter or Space to continue. The shared save data stays local. Use a current desktop Chrome or Edge browser.
